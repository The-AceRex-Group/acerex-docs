# The Warzone

The Warzone is the unclaimed area surrounding spawn. It's the first stretch of land you'll cross when leaving the safety of spawn, and it's one of the most dangerous areas on the server. This page covers what the Warzone is, what to expect there, and how to get through it safely.

---

## What is the Warzone?

The Warzone is a ring of unclaimed territory immediately outside the spawn safe zone. Unlike spawn, **PvP is fully enabled** here, and because it's unclaimed, **no faction can build, claim, or set up protected bases** in it.

Think of it as the buffer between the safety of spawn and the open wilderness where factions claim and build.

| Area | PvP | Claims Allowed | Safe to Store Items? |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Spawn** | No | No (admin-protected) | Yes |
| **Warzone** | Yes | No | No |
| **Wilderness** | Yes | Yes | Only inside your claims |

---

## Why the Warzone is Dangerous

Every player heading out from spawn — and every player heading back — has to pass through the Warzone. This makes it a natural hotspot for PvP, for a few reasons:

- **High traffic.** New players, returning players, and traders all pass through here constantly, making it an easy place to find targets
- **No claims means no protection.** If you die here, your items drop on the ground for anyone to grab
- **Geared players camp it.** Experienced players sometimes sit in the Warzone specifically to ambush players leaving spawn with starter kits or returning with valuables
- **It's unclaimed, so it's contested.** No faction "owns" this land, and no one is obligated to leave you alone

---

## What Happens If You Die Here

Dying in the Warzone works like dying anywhere else outside your own claimed territory:

- Your inventory drops on the ground at your death location
- Anyone nearby can pick up your dropped items
- You lose power (see the [Power](power) page for details)
- You'll respawn at spawn and need to cross the Warzone again to retrieve anything — if it's even still there

> ⚠️ Don't bring anything you can't afford to lose when passing through the Warzone, especially early on.

---

## Tips for Crossing the Warzone Safely

**Travel light on your first trips out.** Starter kit items are usually replaceable — losing them isn't a big deal. Don't carry valuables, spawners, or important gear through the Warzone unless necessary.

**Move quickly.** Don't linger, build, or fight unless you have to. The goal is to get through, not to stop and explore.

**Travel with faction members if possible.** A group crossing together is much harder to pick off than a lone player.

**Watch for ambushes near the spawn exit.** The area immediately outside spawn's safe zone border is the most heavily camped — players know this is where everyone has to pass through.

**Use `/f map`** as soon as you're able, to understand exactly where the Warzone ends and claimable wilderness begins.

**If you're geared and confident**, the Warzone can also be an opportunity — new players leaving spawn with starter kits are common, low-risk targets. Just be aware this cuts both ways.

---

## Returning to Spawn

The same risks apply on the way back. If you're returning with valuable loot — from raiding, mining, or trading — consider:

- Using `/f home` if your base is close enough to spawn that you don't need to pass through the Warzone at all for most trips
- Coordinating with faction members to escort you back if you're carrying something valuable
- Avoiding peak online hours if the Warzone tends to be heavily camped at certain times

---

## Quick Reference

- **PvP:** Always on
- **Claims:** Not allowed
- **Item drops on death:** Yes — fully lootable by anyone
- **Location:** Surrounds spawn, between the safe zone and the claimable wilderness
- **Best practice:** Move quickly, travel light, travel in groups when possible
